Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it sets no cookies of its own.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the email address, subject and message you enter are sent to Formspree, which forwards them to me by email. I use them only to reply to you. Formspree's own privacy policy covers how it handles the submission.

Fonts and hosting

The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font is requested. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Neither is used by me to identify or track you.
